Small Games with Big Heart

Happyland Games is a two-person indie studio from Greece, creating small games with big heart. Blending cozy aesthetics with clever design, we craft experiences that are easy to pick up, hard to put down, and always made with care.

From relaxing idle games to mythological adventures, our work reflects our passion for thoughtful gameplay, visual polish, and emotional connection, built for players who need a little joy in their day.

Our Games

Fishing Corner

A cozy idle fishing getaway at the corner of your screen. Travel the world, catch rare fish, complete your collection, and upgrade your gear across charming diorama-style locations.

Key Features:

  • Sit at the corner of your screen without interrupting your day
  • Idle & Active Fishing
  • Fish Collection Album
  • Quest System & World Map
  • Mini-Upgrades & Baits

Rogue Titan

Defy the Gods in this 3D Roguelike RPG. As Titan Prometheus, you embark on a legendary quest to steal the sacred fire. Explore treacherous dungeons in this turn-based tactical RPG, where every step unveils a new challenge.

Key Features:

  • Master Turn-Based Tactics in grid-based combat
  • Endless Permadeath with procedural generation
  • Fresh 3D Roguelike visual design
  • Deep strategic combat rewarding positioning
